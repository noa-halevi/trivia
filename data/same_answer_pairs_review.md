# Same-Answer Pairs Review

These pairs were flagged during the final uniqueness check because they share the same correct answer or a closely related theme. Some were later removed to make the dataset feel less repetitive during gameplay.

## Pair A: Marie Curie

- Q255: Who was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize? -> Marie Curie
- Q364: Who was the first person to win two Nobel Prizes in different sciences? -> Marie Curie
- Decision: Removed Q364.
- Reason: Kept the simpler first-woman Nobel question and removed the second Marie Curie Nobel question to reduce same-answer repetition.

## Pair B: Mars

- Q178: Which planet is named after the Roman god of war? -> Mars
- Decision: Removed Q178.
- Reason: Kept the Red Planet question and removed the second Mars question to reduce same-answer repetition.

## Pair C: Leonardo da Vinci

- Q6: Which artist painted the 'Mona Lisa'? -> Leonardo da Vinci
